UPPER FLOORS (the floors extension)
When this building's plan entry has "storeys" above 1, also reply with:
  "stairs": {"room": "<a ground room name the stair rises from>"},
  "upper": [ one object per extra storey, in storey order:
    {"storey": 2,
     "rooms": [{"name": "...", "share": "small|medium|large", "purpose": "..."}],
     "connections": [["Room A", "Room B"]],
     "furniture": [{"template": "catalogue name", "room": "room name", "count": 1, "hint": "..."}]}
  ]
- The stair rises from the named ground room; every storey's stair sits at
  the same spot, so the room you name must have floor to spare. The code
  places the stair objects and the game links them storey to storey as a
  two-way passage people and NPCs walk through; you only name the room the
  stair rises from.
- People who live upstairs sleep there: their bedrooms move up a storey, one
  bed per sleeper. Furnish the upper rooms for their purpose — a landing has
  a lamp, a study has a desk.
- Do not list stairs, staircases or a ladder up as furniture: the code
  places the real stairs itself, and a stair of your own would lead nowhere.
  A ruined building is the one exception — a stair rising to a collapsed
  floor belongs in its furnishings.
