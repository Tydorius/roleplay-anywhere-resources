- lootPools: named lists written for THIS setting, never generic: by room kind
  (a bathroom is not a garage) and by building or shop kind (a clothing store,
  a pharmacy and a forge each get their own). Fill them from the menu. One item
  may sit in many pools. "weights" (item -> relative chance, most 1.0) are
  optional. Use at least 25 different items across all pools.
- variants: a menu item under a new name, with a new colour (red, orange,
  yellow, green, teal, blue, purple, pink, brown, black) and its own sentence.
  Use them for local flavour: a guild's blue ledger, a green potion, a red
  scarf. Items marked "recolourable" take a colour best. Variants cost nothing
  to draw; use them freely.
- items: invent a NEW item only when the story needs something the menu and
  variants cannot give: a quest object, a named heirloom, a faction token, a
  unique key, a relic. Mark each "plot": true. Each new item needs its own
  picture drawn, so never invent ordinary things (food, tools, clothes, books,
  coins) here. Most worlds need only a few. "value" is a whole number on the
  game's scale: one coin is about a US dollar.
