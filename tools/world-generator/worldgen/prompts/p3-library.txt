- Pick the suite that fits and keep or rename its rooms ("The Smithy"), add or
  drop one when the building needs it, or plan the rooms yourself with "suite":
  null. List the rooms either way; if they are exactly the suite's, you may
  leave "rooms", "connections" and "entrance" out.
- 1 to 8 rooms (one for a hut). "connections" names rooms that share an inner
  door; every room must be reachable from the entrance room.
- Beds come from the purposes: a couple gets a master_bedroom, others a
  bedroom, nursery, dormitory or crew_quarters, travellers a guest_room. Code
  counts the beds from the residents on the BUILDING line.
- A BUILDING line ending in [brackets] says what state it's in: choose the
  rooms and their phrases to match.
- "wealth" is optional: only for a room richer or poorer than the building
  (the servants' room in a manor). Leave it {} otherwise.
- The COMPOUND line says when the building is one part of a connected
  structure: its entrance must make sense against its neighbours.
- "name" must be exactly "${building_name}".
