# The Sundered Boundary: The Forgotten Village

A xianxia "weakest village" scenario for Anywhere RPG. You are **Wu Ye**, one mouth among forty-three
families in a village with no name on any map, living alone in the last house before the tree line.
Last night a pillar of light stood in the eastern mountains. The elders say it was nothing. This
morning the birds are silent, the thin qi feels disturbed, and strangers have begun to arrive.

**Import:** in the game, choose **New Game → Import…** and pick
`Sundered-Boundary-Forgotten-Village.scenario.zip`. All images are included (portraits, creatures,
items, perks and map objects), so nothing needs generating at Start Game.

## The valley

- **The village:** houses in three rows along Main Street and the Back Lane, the one stone well
  in the middle of everything, the threshing floor behind it, the earth lord's shrine, the
  hunters' lodge, and your house on the east edge, facing the trees.
- **The fields:** terraced millet to the north-west, the lower fields below the track, and the
  south fields below the village.
- **The ridge:** the ancestor stones, a slope of wild herbs, and an abandoned charcoal burners'
  hut someone has swept out.
- **The Westward Track** runs west to a roadside waystation and, two and a half days on, Heiyuan
  City. Leaving down it ends this chapter.
- **The woods:** a safe tree line where the hunters set snares, and beyond it the **Forbidden
  Forest** to the east and the **Northern Deep Woods**, with a cave nobody talks about. The
  dangerous ground is well clear of the village, so trouble comes when you go looking for it.

## Systems

- **Variables:** `Qi` (0 to 100; at the top, the first meridian strains to open), `Realm` (you
  start Mortal), `Karma`, `Satiety`, and a sick man's remaining breath.
- **Perk trees:** Thin Soil, Deep Roots (*Stubborn Back → Count Everything → The Elders Said It
  Was Fine*), The Disturbed Breath (*Feel the Thin Qi → Breath Counting → Open the First
  Meridian*), and Forest-Edge Hunter (*Read the Tree Line → Snare Line → Still as Pine*).
- **Calendar:** morning water at the well, field work, the evening gathering at the threshing
  floor, a weekly offering at the shrine, tonight's elders' council, and two departures in the
  first week that will not wait for you.
- **Quests:** *The Cough in the Mist* and *The Pillar in the East*.
- **Currency:** copper cash. You start with 12, a foraging knife, dried roots, a cracked spirit
  stone and a carved keepsake token.

## Tone

Poverty is counted (one well, one cart, four working ploughs), cultivation does not come easily,
and realm decides who bows. Nobody explains the pillar.
