# The Sundered Boundary: Royal Masquerade, The Entrance Ceremony

An otome-isekai scenario for Anywhere RPG. You wake as **Elise Marlowe**, a minor noble in her first
week at the Aurex Athenaeum, a magic academy whose story you already know from a book you read in
another life, along with every doom flag waiting in it.

**Import:** in the game, choose **New Game → Import…** and pick
`Sundered-Boundary-Royal-Masquerade.scenario.zip`. All images are included (portraits, items,
perks and map objects, made with Flux.2 klein), so nothing needs generating at Start Game.

## The setting

- **Campus:** the Solstice Courtyard and its 56-week sundial at the centre. Theory Hall (crimson)
  east, Elemental Range (sapphire) west, and Knights' Hall (emerald) south with the Proving Yard.
  North across the Processional Way stands the Great Hall, between the chapel and the infirmary,
  with the Alchemical Labs (obsidian) and the Ossian Library behind it. The Interdict is a black
  tower on the north-east corner. The dormitory houses line the south wall, and the gardens run
  down the western slope. Past the wards lies the Borderwood.
- **Daily life:** Knights rise at 5, the refectory serves at 6:30, 12:30 and 18:30, and curfew is
  at 22.
- **The cast keeps secrets.** Lady Sabine Vayne is investigating the Vesperine Order. Alys Wren
  has light magic. Headmaster Corvane guards a sealed treatise. A magister is not what he seems.
  Two of the Concord's summoned other-world champions, Mira Delaney and Desmond Faraday, are
  here too: mirrors for an isekai player.
- **Doom flags:** the public slight (week 9), the betrothal dispute (week 19), the obstruction
  (week 24), the hour (week 48) and graduation (week 50). The game master knows how each can be
  lowered.

## Systems

- **Variables:** `AcademicWeek`, `Reputation`, `Magic`, `Knowledge`, `Social` (all start at 20),
  `Mana` (the local field, regenerating), and text trackers for `Hearts` and `DoomFlags`.
- **Perk trees:** The Reader's Advantage (genre savvy: *I Know This Scene → Doom-Flag Sense →
  Rewrite the Chapter*), Ice and Frost, and Court Graces.
- **Calendar:** today's entrance ceremony and welcome reception; daily lectures and practicums
  from day 2; refectory supper and curfew; weekly vespers; club recruitment (day 8), Council
  elections (day 22) and first assessments (day 29).
- **Quests:** *The Shape of the Story* and *First Week at the Athenaeum*.
- **Time:** the academic year is 56 *weeks*, but the game runs in days. The game master advances
  `AcademicWeek` as the story moves rather than waiting out every day.

## Playing someone else

Elise starts with 60 Thalers, a season presentation card and **an unopened letter**, enrolled in
Elemental Mastery. To change her, edit the player in the New Game screen before starting: for
example, a scholarship student with a plain uniform, a grey guest card and 15 Thalers.
